Facility Admin — Screen inventory

  • FA-01 Dashboard (this screen)
  • FA-02 Appointment list — filterable by dept, doctor, date, status
  • FA-03 Live queue manager — all departments, drag-to-reorder, promote/demote
  • FA-04 Doctor management — add/remove doctors, link to departments, set schedule
  • FA-05 Insurance management — toggle accepted insurers, update coverage dates
  • FA-06 Walk-in intake — search patient or create new, select dept, assign queue
  • FA-07 Reports — daily/weekly/monthly, downloadable CSV/PDF
  • FA-08 Facility profile — hours, services, contact, location (shown on patient app)
  • FA-09 Subscription & billing — plan, payment history, MTN MoMo
  • FA-10 Staff management — add receptionist accounts, set role permissions

Key interactions & business rules

  • Promote button on urgent patient — moves to top of queue, sends push to patient
  • Mark no-show → slot freed → system auto-notifies next waitlisted patient
  • Check in required for queue position to activate (patient physically present)
  • KPI cards clickable — drill down to filtered appointment list
  • Hourly flow chart: WebSocket update every 5 min, line animates smoothly
  • Export report: PDF includes facility header, date range, all tables and charts
  • Role restriction: Receptionist sees FA-03 and FA-06 only (no reports, no billing)
  • Department head sees only their department's appointments and queue

Admin UX rules

  • Dark theme — reduces eye strain for internal power users
  • All destructive actions (suspend, delete) require typed confirmation: type facility name to confirm
  • Role elevation requires 2-admin approval — cannot be self-approved
  • Audit log is append-only — no admin can delete or edit log entries
  • ML model retrain shows estimated completion time and locks retraining button until done
  • PDPL deletion requests have a 7-day countdown before hard delete executes
  • System health dashboard auto-refreshes every 60 seconds
  • All approval actions send email notification to requester

MoH portal screen inventory

  • MH-01 National overview (this screen)
  • MH-02 District drill-down — click district → all facilities in that district
  • MH-03 Facility performance ranking — sortable, filterable by type
  • MH-04 Disease/symptom trends — what conditions patients report most (anonymised)
  • MH-05 Insurance coverage analytics — Mutuelle vs RAMA vs MMI reach
  • MH-06 Wait time trends — monthly, by facility type, by district
  • MH-07 Data export center — PDF, CSV, for ministerial reports

Data privacy constraints (hard rules)

  • Zero individual patient data — all figures are aggregated minimum group of 10
  • No names, phone numbers, or booking IDs ever displayed
  • Export files automatically strip any field that could identify an individual
  • MoH user login: 2FA mandatory, session timeout 30 minutes of inactivity
  • All MoH data access is logged in the compliance audit trail
  • District health officer sees only their district's data — enforced at API level
